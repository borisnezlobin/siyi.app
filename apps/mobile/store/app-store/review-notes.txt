siyi.app is a private people-and-follow-up organizer. It does not scrape Instagram or any other social network.

The review account should be preloaded with fictional people, updates, birthdays, and follow-ups. No real person data should be used.

Account deletion is available in Settings > Delete account and data. Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are available before sign-in and from Settings.

Push notification permission is requested only after the reviewer taps Set up notifications during onboarding or Enable push notifications on the Notifications screen. Remote push requires a physical device. The Send a test notification action is available after permission is granted.

Sign in with Apple and Google require network access. Email/password is available as a reliable review fallback. After the first successful load, cached people, notes, updates, follow-ups, and photos remain available offline, and changes sync when connectivity returns.
