siyi.app is a private people-and-follow-up organizer.

Instagram profile pictures
When the user saves someone and has entered that person's Instagram username, the app requests that profile's public picture from Instagram and offers it as the saved person's photo. The request is made only for a username the user typed, only at the moment they save that person, and only the picture is read. No Instagram account is required, nothing is posted, and leaving the username blank skips the lookup entirely. The user can decline the offered picture or replace it later.

Review account
Email: ${APP_REVIEW_EMAIL}
Password: ${APP_REVIEW_PASSWORD}

The review account should be preloaded with fictional people, updates, birthdays, and follow-ups. No real person data should be used.

Account deletion is available in Settings > Delete account and data. Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are available before sign-in and from Settings.

Push notification permission is requested only after the reviewer taps Set up notifications during onboarding or Enable push notifications on the Notifications screen. Remote push requires a physical device. The Send a test notification action is available after permission is granted.

Contacts permission is optional and is never requested at launch. It is offered only after the reviewer saves their first person, behind an in-app explanation shown before the system prompt, and declining leaves every feature working. It is used to match a saved person to an existing contact and to add saved people to the address book on request. The address book is read on the device and is never uploaded to our servers.

A new account starts with a shareable card carrying its own full name and major, reachable at an unlisted, non-indexed link. It is the account holder's own card, never any of the people they have saved. Under Your card the reviewer can choose which fields it shows, or switch the link off entirely, at which point the page stops resolving.

Sign in with Apple and Google require network access. Email/password is available as a reliable review fallback. After the first successful load, cached people, notes, updates, follow-ups, and photos remain available offline, and changes sync when connectivity returns.
